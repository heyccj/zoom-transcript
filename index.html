How it works under the hood

ZT Captions doesn't transcribe anything itself. It piggybacks on Zoom's own native closed captioning — the live transcription Carnegie already enables for accessibility. All the bookmarklet does is read those captions as they appear, then use a bit of JavaScript to save, download, and organize them into a clean transcript with speakers and timestamps. The words come straight from Zoom; we just keep a tidy copy.
